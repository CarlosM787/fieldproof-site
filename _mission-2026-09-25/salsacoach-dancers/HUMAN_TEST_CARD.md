## C. Instructor review (one merged list)

The dancers' 7 questions and the footwork teacher's 6, with duplicates merged. Carlos chooses the instructor; nobody is contacted on his behalf.

1. [ ] **Which count is "On2"?** The 2-3-4 count (what the app scores), the Torres count, or both with clear names?
2. [ ] **2-3-4 count:** does the leader break **back** or **forward** on 2?
3. [ ] **Foot timing:** here the foot leaves the floor at 0.6 of the beat and the weight lands **on** the count. Should the step start on the "and", or the weight arrive slightly before?
4. [ ] **The pause** (4 and 8, or 1 and 5): no step and no tap here. Should it be a tap, a hold, or either?
5. [ ] **Replace step:** a weight change in place, with no travel. Is that right for a beginner?
6. [ ] **Step size:** the break is 24 cm and the partners are 42 cm apart, ankle to ankle. Should the slow teaching mode use larger steps?
7. [ ] **Heels:** forward steps land flat, back steps land on the ball and then lower the heel, and a free back foot rolls onto its ball. Is that what you teach?
8. [ ] **Hips and body** (generated): the pelvis shifts about 4.5 cm over the standing leg, then settles 1.2 cm more after the weight lands, and the chest turns slightly against it. Too much, too little, or wrong timing?
9. [ ] **Hold for beginners:** the joined hands are at her shoulder height, 45 cm to his left; his right hand is on her shoulder blade; her left hand is on his shoulder. Change anything?
10. [ ] **Default view:** behind the student's own role, or facing with a mirror?

New in phase three (quick yes/no):
- [ ] "Quick, quick, slow" is how you would name the rhythm.
- [ ] The count voice on every count (softer on the pause) helps a beginner.
