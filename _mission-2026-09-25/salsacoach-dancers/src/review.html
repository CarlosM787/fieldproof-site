What changed since phase two

The count engine is unchanged: its code is byte-identical to phase two, and every phase-two check still runs. On top of it: generated body motion, teaching overlays, half-speed and step-by-step modes, a count voice, and the five-step practice above. Generated motion, not motion capture. Not reviewed by an instructor. Not tested on a real phone.

The practice flow, rendered

  1. Listen. Two 8-counts with a click on every 1. The dancers load in the background (the 3D loads only after this tap).
  2. Find the 1. The counts are hidden; tap on every 1. The Count Lab judge: ±110 ms is "On the 1", and it says so when you tap the 5. Three in a row finish the step.
  3. Watch slowly. Half speed with every overlay; "Step by step" goes one count per tap or key press.
  4. Step along. Full speed, the count voice on every count (softer on the pause), the big number flashing, and the step for your role in words.
  5. Speed up. ±10 BPM or presets from 110 to 190. The last tempo is the only thing the page stores.
