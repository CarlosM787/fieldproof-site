Angle (filmed takes only)

150 BPM take. Speeds between 0.75× and 1.25× keep the music's pitch and still look natural.

  • Leader or follower alone: needs separate solo takes.
  • On2: needs its own takes; a video cannot re-time a dancer's feet.
  • Any other angle: only the angles that were filmed.
Capture plan for real footage (half a day, not booked)
  • Two real dancers (ideally the SalsaCoach instructor and a partner), paid, with signed model releases covering web, app and advertising. Never use other people's footage.
  • Three phones on tripods recording at the same time (behind the leader, behind the follower, side), 4K 60 fps, fixed exposure. Tape marks on the floor for home and break positions; a clap slate at the start of each take.
  • The synthesized band played at a fixed tempo, so every take shares a beat grid and the count overlay is exact.
  • 27 takes: 3 conventions × 3 tempos (90, 150, 190 BPM) × 3 role variants (couple, leader alone, follower alone), about 30 seconds each, plus spares.
  • A hybrid worth pricing: markerless motion capture of the instructor, retargeted onto these avatars. It keeps every 3D control and adds real styling, but needs a paid tool and Carlos's decision.

Two dancers step on every count of the band, at practice speed or full speed. Follow the footprints until your feet do it without you.

Practice the basicHow the count works
  • The weight lands on the count. A foot leaves the floor late in the beat and lands exactly on it, so the eye learns where the beat is.
  • The glow shows the weight. The footprint under the standing foot lights up, so the question "which foot now?" answers itself.
  • Partners mirror. When the leader breaks forward, the follower breaks back, and the couple moves as one.
  • Light on phones. The poster is about 30 KB. The 3-second loop is about 225 KB, and it loads only when the banner is on screen, never with data saver or reduced motion on.
