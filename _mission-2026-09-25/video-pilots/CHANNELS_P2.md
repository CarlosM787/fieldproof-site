# Which channel? Eight more faceless formats, scored against the two pilots

**For:** Carlos Morales Jr. **Date:** 2026-09-26. **Status:** a recommendation only. Nothing was
created, uploaded or bought. **Carlos chooses the channel and the pilot before any upload**, and the
first upload should be *Unlisted*, for a check on his own phone.

Labels used here:
- **REPORTED**: taken from `YOUTUBE_RESEARCH.md` (2026-09-25), which saw official pages only as
  search excerpts.
- **INFERENCE**: my reasoning, not measured.
- **OPEN**: not checked at all. This sandbox reaches neither YouTube nor Google Trends, and the search
  budget is spent, so no demand number below was measured this session.

## 1. Recommendation

**Start one channel: Clave Lab, bilingual salsa timing.** Make it Shorts-first, with one longer
lesson a month. Then decide on a second channel at a gate, after 90 days.

Why one and not several (INFERENCE):
1. **The audiences don't overlap.** Salsa learners and engineering/data viewers are different
   people. A channel that alternates between them teaches the recommendation system that neither
   audience is reliably served.
2. **Every threshold is per channel.** Splitting Carlos's limited hours across two channels delays
   both (see §2). Shorts views also don't count toward watch hours.
3. **Clave Lab is the cheapest to make and the most original.** The v2 pipeline makes a Short from an
   episode spec in about 2.5 minutes of machine time (measured in this container). The "band X-ray"
   and synthesized groove have no Content ID exposure. The format links directly to mysalsacoach.com.
   The coordinator's 3D dancers slot straight into the floor layer (`--floor-dir`).
4. **The data/engineering ideas are better as one later channel than as three small ones.** That
   channel would combine Desert Systems, Desert Home Energy (F-c) and Circuitos Claros (F-a). It
   should wait until Carlos can:
   - record his own voice-overs, which finance-adjacent content needs anyway (the AI-persona rule,
     REPORTED);
   - add about 6–8 hours a week.

   Until then, the Lake Powell work lives more cheaply on X (the bot) and the Substack.

**Gate at day 90.** Continue Clave Lab if these point the right way:
- the Studio's retention curves;
- subscribers per 1,000 views;
- clicks through to mysalsacoach.com;
- email sign-ups.

Open the second channel only if Carlos has the hours and wants the voice work.

Rules that hold for every option:
- **No separate EN and ES channels.** Use bilingual on-screen text or multi-language audio tracks.
  Near-duplicate uploads across channels fall under the spam policy (REPORTED).
- **No AI voices for finance or health topics** (REPORTED).
- **No commercial music.**

## 5. The formats in brief

### F-c Desert Home Energy
Arizona power bills and the electrical engineering behind them:
- how a time-of-use bill is computed;
- what an AC compressor draws at 110 °F;
- solar and EV payback, worked as code-drawn calculations with real rate tables.

**Demand:** OPEN. Summer-bill interest in Arizona is plausible (INFERENCE).

**Original:** high. This is where Carlos's engineering, finance and Tucson all meet.

**Rights:**
- Rate schedules are public facts; snapshot them with dates.
- Avoid utility logos.
- Frame it as education, never as advice.
- Use his own voice (the AI-persona rule).

**Effort:** 8–12 h per episode.

**Business link:** faroquant.com calculators, the Substack, and talkestimate.com for electrical
contractors doing solar or EV work.

**First test:** "Why your July bill doubles: the AC compressor, in watts."

**Best as:** a strand of the future engineering channel.

### F-d Estimating explained
Faceless walk-throughs of how a real trade job is priced:
- materials;
- labour hours;
- overhead;
- margin.

For example, a panel upgrade or an EV charger install.

**Demand:** OPEN (research F8).

**Business link:** the most direct of all, to talkestimate.com.

**Constraint:** talkestimate.com's marketing copy is frozen until the S25 pass. Videos must teach
estimating in general and must not show or claim app features that aren't verified.

**Effort:** 4–8 h.

**Best as:** a recurring segment once talkestimate.com unfreezes.

### F-f Trade Spanish
Bilingual job-site vocabulary Shorts, using the code-drawn diagram kit. For example:
- "breaker / interruptor";
- "conduit / tubo conduit";
- "load calculation / cálculo de carga".

**Demand:** OPEN. A bilingual trades workforce is plausible (INFERENCE).

**Effort:** very low, 0.5–1 h per Short.

**Risk:** the "inauthentic content" rule targets templated, repetitive Shorts (REPORTED). Each one
has to teach a distinct, useful point, so it scores 4, not 5, on policy.

**Business link:** talkestimate.com is bilingual.

**Best as:** a Shorts strand for talkestimate.com later, not a channel of its own.

### F-e Dance mechanics
Why turns work (angular momentum), what "frame" does (force through the arms), and weight transfer.
It uses the Count Lab skeleton, and later the 3D dancers.

**Demand:** OPEN. It's a niche.

**Original:** very high.

**Rights:** clean.

**Effort:** high, 12–18 h. Each episode needs research, biomechanics animation and an instructor
check.

**Business link:** mysalsacoach.com.

**Best as:** an occasional long lesson on the Clave Lab channel. This is the natural long-form
companion to the Shorts.

### F-g Carlos Builds
Screen recordings with his own voice: building real products with AI tools.

**Demand:** probably strong but crowded (INFERENCE).

**Rights:**
- Third-party interfaces on screen are generally fine for commentary (INFERENCE, not legal advice).
- Never show keys, customer data or private repositories.

**Effort:** 4–8 h.

**Business link:** the Substack and every product.

**Best as:** Substack video posts first.

### F-a Circuitos Claros
Electrical engineering in Spanish: Ohm's law to three-phase, power factor, motors. Code-animated
circuits.

**Demand evidence:** QuantumFracture, Spanish physics animation, has about 3.86M subscribers
(REPORTED, third-party source). Animated electrical engineering in Spanish looks lightly covered
(INFERENCE).

**Rights:** explain standards; don't copy their text.

**Effort:** 10–14 h.

**Best as:** the Spanish audio track and Spanish Shorts of the engineering channel, not a separate
channel (research §1.10–1.11).

### F-h Money math, animated
Compound interest, amortization, fees, inflation.

**Demand:** high. How Money Works has about 1.75M subscribers (REPORTED, third-party source).

**Competition:** heavy.

**Rights and policy:**
- It is a "your money" topic: Carlos's own voice only.
- Education, not advice.

**Original:** low.

**Business link:** faroquant.com.

**Not recommended.** Carlos's edge doesn't show in a crowded field.

### F-b FE Electrical exam prep
Original worked problems, animated, in EN and ES.

**Demand:** OPEN.

**Rights:**
- The problems must be his own. Official exam materials are copyrighted.
- Never imply endorsement by the exam body.

**Business link:** weak. It would need a new product, such as problem packs.

**Not recommended now.**
